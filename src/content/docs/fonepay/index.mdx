[Fonepay](https://fonepay.com) is Nepal's interbank QR payment network. Customers pay from the mobile banking apps and wallets of member institutions, and merchants receive the money in their own account.

| Product | Use it when | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Dynamic QR** | You want to show a one-time QR for each order | [Dynamic QR overview](dynamic-qr/overview/) |
| **Checkout by Fonepay** | You want a bank-picker and app-to-app payment flow | [Checkout overview](checkout/overview/) |

New to this? Read [Choose a product](../getting-started/overview/) and [Get an account and credentials](../getting-started/onboarding/) first.

:::caution[Check the source documents]
These guides are based on Fonepay's official documents: **Dynamic QR "Merchant Integration through Device for online QR"** and **Checkout Intent Flow v1.10 (May 2026)**. Vendor documents change. Always confirm against the latest version from Fonepay. Known contradictions are listed on the [known issues](known-issues/) page.
:::
