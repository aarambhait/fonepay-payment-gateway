Fonepay published **"Checkout by Fonepay Brand & Identity Guidelines 2026"** for merchants. This is a summary of the rules. It is **not** a substitute for the original. Request the official brand assets and guidelines from Fonepay, and do not recreate the logo yourself.

## Colours

| Name | HEX | RGB |
|---|---|---|
| Fonepay Red | `#ce2027` | 206, 32, 39 |
| Fonepay Black | `#0a0d12` | 10, 13, 18 |
| Fonepay White | `#ffffff` | 255, 255, 255 |

Don't use other colours in the logo, and don't use gradients.

## Logo rules

- Keep **clear space** around the logo equal to the height of the "o" in "fone" on every side. In tight UI such as buttons you may reduce this to 0.5x, but no less. Marketing banners should use 1x to 1.5x.
- **Don't**: use the logo without its box, rotate it, rearrange it, outline it, stretch or squeeze it, change its proportions or use unspecified colours.
- **Minimum sizes**: the guideline mentions 45 px (general, logo height), 35 px (desktop preview) and 30 px (banking-app preview). These conflict, so ask Fonepay which applies to you, or use the largest if unsure.
- In running text, write **"Checkout by Fonepay"**.

## Desktop checkout screen

- Place the **Fonepay network sign before** the Checkout by Fonepay logo.
- Centre the logo with the QR code, and include the Fonepay network brandmark on the merchant's QR.
- Show a red **Check Status** button and a short "how to pay" step list.
- Suggested heading text: "Scan & Pay Easily with Your Mobile Banking Apps or Wallets".

## Bank selection screen

- A **search bar at the top** of the list.
- List banks and wallets with their logos. Never squeeze or stretch bank logos.
- After selection, the customer is redirected to their bank app, reviews the details, taps **Confirm** and enters their PIN or biometrics.

## Error state

If the chosen app isn't available, show a message such as:

> Your selected mobile banking app or wallet isn't available right now. Please choose another option to continue your payment.

Fonepay's mock-up titles it "Mobile banking App not found" and offers a **Try Again** action.
