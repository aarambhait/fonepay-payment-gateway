Fonepay does not publish its developer documentation or a self-serve sandbox on its public website. You get both through onboarding. This page explains the path as far as it can be verified, and tells you what to ask for.

:::note[Last verified]
Contact details and the enrollment form below were checked on **26 November 2026** at [fonepay.com](https://fonepay.com). They can change, so check the official site before you rely on them.
:::

## Step 1: Become a Fonepay merchant

Fonepay QR is issued through partner banks and wallets. There are two entry points:

1. **Enrollment form.** Submit the "Request Fonepay QR" form at [fonepay.com/business/merchant-enrollment](https://fonepay.com/business/merchant-enrollment). It asks for:
   - the owner's full name, citizenship number, mobile number and email
   - business name, city, and business type (retail, restaurant or cafe, service, e-commerce, other)
   - whether the business is **PAN** or **non-PAN**
   - your preferred bank or e-wallet for QR issuance
2. **Through your bank.** Fonepay's own pages tell merchants to contact their bank or the Fonepay team to integrate. If you already bank with a Fonepay member bank, start with its merchant services team.

The enrollment page does not list the supporting documents you will need. Ask your bank or Fonepay for the current list.

## Step 2: Ask for integration access

Once you are a merchant, tell your bank or Fonepay that you want to **integrate online**, and say which product you want. Ask for:

<ul>
  <li>The <strong>official API document</strong> for your product (check the version number and date).</li>
  <li><strong>Test (UAT / dev) credentials</strong> and the test base URLs.</li>
  <li><strong>Production credentials</strong> and base URLs, and the go-live process.</li>
  <li>The <strong>IP allowlist</strong> process, if any, for your servers.</li>
</ul>

### What you will receive

| Product | Typical credentials |
|---|---|
| Dynamic QR | `merchantCode`, `username`, `password`, and a **secret key** shown on your merchant profile page after you log in |
| Checkout by Fonepay | `username`, `password`, `terminalId`. You generate an RSA key pair and share the **public key** with Fonepay |

Treat all of these as secrets. See [security best practices](../../guides/security/).

## A message template that gets answers

When you contact your bank or Fonepay, being specific saves rounds of back and forth:

```text
Subject: Online integration request - <your business name> (merchant code <code if you have it>)

Hello,

We are a Fonepay merchant and want to accept online payments on our website/app.
We would like to integrate <Dynamic QR | Checkout by Fonepay>.

Please share:
1. The latest official API document and version.
2. Test (UAT) credentials and base URLs.
3. How and where to find / submit our keys (secret key or public key).
4. The production base URLs and your go-live checklist.
5. Whether our server IPs must be allowlisted.
6. The tax refund (IRD) reporting requirement for Dynamic QR, if it applies to us.

Thank you.
```
